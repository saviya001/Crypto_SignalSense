# Ethereum (ETH) Asset Profile & Fundamentals

## Fundamental Characteristics
- **Role:** Premier smart-contract platform, DeFi backbone, Layer-2 ecosystem foundation.
- **Key Drivers:** ETH staking yields, EIP-1559 gas burn mechanism, Layer-2 TVL growth (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base).
- **Correlation:** ETH/BTC ratio indicates altcoin season momentum. High ETH strength favors altcoin long positions.
