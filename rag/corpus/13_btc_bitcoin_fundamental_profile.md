# Bitcoin (BTC) Asset Profile & Fundamentals

## Fundamental Characteristics
- **Role:** Digital gold, macro store of value, primary crypto market benchmark.
- **Key Metrics:** Bitcoin Dominance (BTC.D), 4-year Halving Cycle, Institutional Spot ETF Inflows.
- **Trading Bias:** BTC leads the market trend. Altcoin signals (ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP) must be aligned with overall BTC trend direction.
