# Support & Resistance Zones Identification

## Concepts
- **Support Zone:** Price level where buying interest is strong enough to overcome selling pressure, causing price to bounce upwards.
- **Resistance Zone:** Price level where selling pressure overcomes buying interest, halting or reversing an upward trend.
- **Role Reversals:** Once broken, former support becomes resistance, and former resistance becomes support.

## Strategy
Always place BUY orders near strong support zones with Stop-Loss just below support. Never buy directly under a major resistance level.
