# BNB (Binance Ecosystem Token) Asset Profile & Fundamentals

## Fundamental Characteristics
- **Role:** Utility token powering BNB Chain, DEX transactions, and Binance launchpads.
- **Key Metrics:** Real-time gas burns, BNB Chain daily active addresses, Launchpool announcements.
- **Volatility Profile:** Lower beta compared to smaller caps, supported by consistent exchange utility demand.
