# Crypto Market Sentiment & News Analysis Framework

## Sentiment Classification Metrics
- **Bullish (+1.0):** ETF approvals, institutional buying, major network upgrades, regulatory clarity.
- **Neutral (0.0):** Standard developments, routine updates, balanced macro environment.
- **Bearish (-1.0):** Exchange hacks, regulatory bans, macro rate hikes, liquidations cascade.

## Sentiment Confluence
If overall news sentiment is strongly Bearish (-1.0), BUY signals must be flagged with lower confidence or converted to HOLD.
