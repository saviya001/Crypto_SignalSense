# Take-Profit Strategy & Profit Scaling

## Targets Structure
- **TP1 (Primary Target):** Set at nearest key resistance level or 1.5x risk distance. Close 50% of position and move Stop Loss to breakeven.
- **TP2 (Extended Target):** Set at major liquidity pool, Fibonacci 0.618 level, or 3.0x risk distance for maximum trend capture.
