# Multi-Timeframe Trend Confluence Strategy

## Strategy Breakdown
- **Macro Trend (Daily 1D):** Determines overall direction (Bullish, Bearish, Sideways).
- **Execution Setup (4-Hour 4H):** Identifies key support/resistance structures and pattern formations.
- **Entry Trigger (1-Hour 1H):** Pinpoints precise entry price with tight stop loss.

Rule: Never enter a 1H Buy signal if the 1D Daily trend is strongly Bearish.
