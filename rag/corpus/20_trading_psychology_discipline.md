# Trading Psychology & Emotional Discipline Rules

## Core Rules
1. **No Over-trading:** Maximum 3 active crypto signals at a time.
2. **Accept Losses Gracefully:** Stop-Loss is non-negotiable; never widen or remove a Stop-Loss during an active trade.
3. **Avoid FOMO:** Never buy at the top of a green candle after price has already moved up >5% in a short period. Wait for pullback.
