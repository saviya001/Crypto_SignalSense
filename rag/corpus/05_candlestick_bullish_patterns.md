# Bullish Candlestick Reversal Patterns Playbook

## Patterns
1. **Bullish Engulfing:** A small red candle followed by a large green candle that completely covers the previous body. High reliability at support levels.
2. **Hammer:** Small body at upper end with a long lower wick (at least 2x body length). Indicates strong buying pressure rejecting lower prices.
3. **Morning Star:** A 3-candle pattern starting with a long red candle, a small indecision star candle, and a strong green candle closing past 50% of the first candle.

## Rule
Candlestick patterns MUST be confirmed at key support levels or oversold RSI zones. Never trade candlestick patterns in isolation.
