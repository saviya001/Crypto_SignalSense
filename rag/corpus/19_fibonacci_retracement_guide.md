# Fibonacci Retracement Strategy Guide

## Key Fibonacci Ratio Levels
- **0.382 Level:** Shallow retracement level in extremely strong momentum trends.
- **0.500 Level:** Psychological midpoint retracement.
- **0.618 Level (Golden Pocket):** High probability reversal zone for entering long positions in an uptrend.

## Execution Rules
Look for confluence where the 0.618 Fibonacci level aligns with horizontal support and dynamic 50 SMA.
