# Stop-Loss Placement & Calculation Guide

## Execution Rules
1. **Long Position (BUY):** Place Stop Loss 1% - 2% below recent swing low or technical support level.
2. **Short Position (SELL):** Place Stop Loss 1% - 2% above recent swing high or technical resistance level.
3. **Volatility Adjustment:** During high market volatility, expand stop loss distance using ATR (Average True Range) multipliers (e.g. 1.5x ATR) to avoid premature stop-outs.
